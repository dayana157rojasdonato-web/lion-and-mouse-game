🦁 The Lion and the Mouse 🐭

An adventure of friendship, courage and kindness!

⭐ Level: 1/3
❤️ Lives: 3
🧀 Cheese: 0

🐭 Help the Little Mouse!

The little mouse is lost in the forest. Help him collect all the cheese, avoid dangerous traps, hide from the lion and find the exit!

Be careful! The lion is chasing you!

⌨️ Use ARROW KEYS or W, A, S, D to move.
🧀 Collect ALL the cheese before reaching the exit.
🕳️ Stand near a hole and press E or SPACE to hide.
🔓 Press E or SPACE again to leave the hiding place.
⚠️ Avoid rocks, logs and traps. Do not let the lion catch you!
